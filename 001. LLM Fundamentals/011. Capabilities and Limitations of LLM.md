Finally, let's talk about what LLMs can do and cannot do (or cannot do perfectly).

LLMs are powerful because they are very good at pattern-based language prediction. But that also explains many of their limits.

# CAPABILITIES

LLMs can do a lot of useful things because they learned patterns from huge amounts of text.

They are good at:

    - explaining concepts
    - summarizing text
    - translating languages
    - rewriting in different tones
    - answering many factual questions
    - writing code
    - debugging code
    - brainstorming ideas
    - extracting structured information
    - comparing options
    - role-playing workflows, like interviewer, tutor, reviewer, or planner
    -reasoning through problems step by step

LLMs are general-purpose text reasoning engines. They are especially strong when the task is language heavy, pattern-based, well-described in the prompt and similar to the examples seen during training.

# LIMITATIONS

But LLMs do not “know” things the way humans do. They generate likely text based on patterns. That creates some important limits.

## 1. LLMs CAN HALLUCINATE

An LLM may produce an answer that sounds confident but is false.

For example -

    It may invent a book title, fake a citation, or give the wrong date.

Why? Because its job is to generate plausible next tokens, not directly verify reality.

## 2. THEY MAY BE OUTDATED

A model’s training data has a cutoff. It may not know recent events, current prices, new laws, new APIs, or today’s news unless connected to tools or browsing.

## 3. THEY DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY UNDERSTAND TRUTH

They can explain true things and false things with the same fluent style. Fluency is not proof of correctness.

## 4. LIMITED CONTEXT

They only see what fits inside the context window. If important information is outside the prompt/context, they may miss it.

## 5. THEY CAN BE SENSITIVE TO WORKING

Small changes in the prompt can sometimes lead to different answers.

## 6. THEY DO NOT HAVE HUMAN EXPERIENCE

They can talk about emotions, taste, ethics, culture, and judgment, but they do not experience the world like a person.

## 7. THEY REFLECT BIAS FROM TRAINING DATA

Because they learn from human-written data, they can inherit stereotypes, gaps, or skewed assumptions.

## 8. THEY NEED CAREFUL USE FOR HIGH-STAKES AREAS

For medical, legal, financial, safety, or security decisions, LLMs should assist, not replace qualified judgment and verification.

So, to summarize -

    LLMs are excellent at generating, transforming, and reasoning over language.

    They are not guaranteed truth machines.

Best way to use them?

    Use LLMs for speed, drafts, explanations, and reasoning support.

    Use verification for facts, calculations, sources, and high-stakes decisions.

An LLM is a powerful language model that can produce useful reasoning-like behavior, but it still needs context, verification, and human judgment.
